The Heritage of Unani Tradition

Centuries of natural philosophy, botanical harmony, and classical Dawakhana wisdom.

A Classical Legacy

Centuries of Natural Harmony

The Unani System of Medicine (Tibb-e-Unani) is one of the world's most enduring traditions of natural philosophy. Drawing heritage from ancient scholars such as Hippocrates (Buqrat) and Ibn Sina (Avicenna), it blossomed across Persia and India into an intricate, refined science of natural balance.

At its heart lies a profound reverence for Tabiyat (the innate self-regulating power of nature) and the harmony of natural elements: Fire, Air, Water, and Earth. In this system, herbs and botanicals are not mere ingredients, but harmonious tools designed by nature to support equilibrium.

“Traditional knowledge connects generations with the natural world.”

Philosophical Pillars

The Foundation of Unani Thought

Understanding the classical principles that govern botanical selection and traditional preparation.

Mizaj (Temperament)

Every individual and botanical ingredient possesses a natural temperament (Hot, Cold, Moist, Dry) which determines its traditional harmony.

Arkan (Elements)

The four universal natural elements—Earth (Khak), Water (Aab), Air (Hawa), and Fire (Aatish)—that form the physical world.

Tabiyat (Natural Power)

The inherent life force of nature that continuously strives toward equilibrium, supported gently by pure herbal nutrients.

Natural Synergism

Combining complementary herbs (Mufeed & Musleh) so that the properties of one naturally enhance and balance another.

Botanical Purity

Using whole herbs rather than synthetic isolations to preserve the complete matrix of natural compounds.

Living Heritage

A continuous unbroken lineage of Hakim practitioners and Dawakhana establishments dedicated to authenticity.

Classical Forms

Traditional Curation Concepts

General educational overview of classical preparation formats in traditional Unani work.

Joshanda

Joshanda (Decoction)

Coarsely crushed dried botanicals traditionally boiled in water to release natural soluble compounds.

Safoof

Safoof (Powders)

Finely sieved and ground botanical roots and seeds prepared following classical proportions.

Majun

Majun (Electuaries)

Traditional herbal pastes blended with pure honey or natural syrups for gentle preservation.

Arq

Arq (Distillates)

Hydro-distilled floral and herbal waters capturing aromatic, cooling, and soothing properties.
