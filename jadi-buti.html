Botanical Foundations

What is Jadi-Buti?

In Indian tradition, “Jadi-Buti” literally refers to the roots (jadiyan) and aerial herbs (buti) gifted by nature. For thousands of years across the Indian subcontinent and within classical Unani literature, natural plants have served as the cornerstone of traditional wellness, nourishment, and daily care.

Every single part of a plant possesses unique characteristics—its aroma, taste, drying properties, and mineral balance. Traditional Jadi-Buti work is the delicate practice of identifying, collecting, sorting, and curing these botanical materials with meticulous respect for their natural state.

“Kudrat ne har paudhe aur jad mein ek anmol taqat rakhi hai — zaroorat hai toh sirf uski sahi samajh aur riwayat ki.”

— Traditional Herbal Wisdom

Anatomy of Herbs

Key Plant Parts in Jadi-Buti

Traditional classification based on the botanical structures utilized in herbal heritage.

01. Roots

Roots & Rhizomes (Jadiyan)

Underground plant structures naturally rich in dense plant compounds, carefully washed and sun-cured.

02. Seeds

Seeds & Grains (Beej)

The vital core of botanical reproduction, packed with natural oils, proteins, and time-honored goodness.

03. Leaves

Foliage & Leaves (Pattiyan)

Aromatic green leaves dried in covered shade to retain volatile botanical essences and fresh pigments.

04. Flowers

Blossoms & Petals (Phool)

Delicate floral heads harvested at dawn and gently dried to preserve gentle fragrance and soothing balance.

05. Bark

Tree Bark & Stems (Chhaal)

Protective outer layers of medicinal trees harvested sustainably to maintain ecological longevity.

06. Dried Herbs

Whole Dried Botanicals

Complete whole plant specimens curated for specialized classical Unani compound handling.

Authentic Methodology

From Nature to Traditional Use

The disciplined stages that ensure our Jadi-Buti maintains purity, integrity, and authentic character.

Stage 01

Natural Source

Identifying prime botanical habitats and harvesting at the optimal seasonal maturity.

Stage 02

Selection & Sorting

Manual inspection, removal of foreign debris, and grading based on aroma, color, and density.

Stage 03

Preparation

Gentle cleaning, traditional drying, coarse grinding or whole storage in breathable conditions.

Stage 04

Traditional Use

Presenting authentic materials to practitioners and patrons following time-honored heritage.
